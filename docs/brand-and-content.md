# Brand and Content Rules

## Source of truth
Use the supplied 2026 BÉ VUI MẸ KHOẺ brand guideline and customer-provided menu/price references.

## Colors
- Brand green: `#7AAF3E`
- Brand pink: `#EB688A`
- Brand yellow: `#FEDA7B`
- Brand gray: `#68696B` (derived from RGB 104, 105, 107 because the PDF repeats the green HEX in that row)
- Warm cream and charcoal may be used as supporting UI neutrals, not replacements for the brand palette.

## Typography
Use Nunito. Suggested hierarchy:
- Black/900–1000: main headings and key brand emphasis.
- ExtraBold/800: section headings and important labels.
- SemiBold/600: descriptions and detailed information.

## Tone
Warm, positive, safe and friendly. Use simple language and avoid fear-based copy or unsupported claims.

## Logo
Use the supplied logo without distortion, recoloring, rotation or decorative effects. Keep sufficient clear space. Do not redraw the logo with a replacement font.

## Business content restrictions
- The website sells Spa services and kids playground tickets separately.
- Do not show Combo products, combo booking, bundle discounts or combo checkout.
- Do not create Event or Event Management pages.
- The provided playground price image has a “COMBO” column heading. The website must not reuse that heading; it displays **thời lượng** and activity information instead.
- The exact Spa service catalogue/prices are not yet supplied. Use “Thông tin sẽ được cập nhật” or consultation CTA until approved data arrives.
- Do not invent branch address, phone number or opening hours.
- Ticket and menu values from images are initial transcription and need owner confirmation before production.
